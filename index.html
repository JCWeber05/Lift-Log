Privacy and securityWhat this app does with your data
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. This app has no server of its own.
  • Your workouts are stored on this device. If you connect Google Sheets, a copy also goes to a sheet in your own Google account.
  • Everyone who opens this page gets a separate, private copy on their own device. Nobody can see anyone else’s workouts.
  • Your Google script runs under your account. Only someone with your secret token can add to it. The token stays on this device and is never put in backup files.
  • Your browser only lets the app contact its own files, your Google script, and Google Fonts (for the text style).
  • Limit: the app has no passcode of its own. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open it, so keep your phone locked.
