Your file never leaves this computer. The page reads it right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, saved or sent anywhere, and this page can’t make network requests.

How to get your file here

  1. On your phone, open Lift log and go to Settings → Data and backup.
  2. Tap Send to my computer and send the file to yourself (email, AirDrop, Drive, or a message to yourself).
  3. Open it on this computer and drop it above.

The viewer shows a snapshot of when you sent the file. Send a new one any time you want it updated.
